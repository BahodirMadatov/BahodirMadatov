## What I'm building now

**Software Engineering Intern @ [Global Move](https://globalmove.uz)** — *Jun 2026 – present*
Selected for the Young Professional Internships Program (YPIP), funded by the U.S.
Embassy in Tashkent.

I designed and shipped **PCalc**, the AI project-cost calculator now live on the
company's site. A visitor is walked through project type, roles, integrations,
compliance and timeline in Uzbek, Russian or English, and gets a transparent estimate
at the end.

The design decision I care about most: **the LLM never sets the price.** Costs come
from a deterministic, versioned pricing module built on the company's fixed Function
Price List. The model explains scope and asks good questions; the arithmetic is
auditable and reproducible. Estimates are also consent-gated — nothing is revealed and
no lead is forwarded until the visitor explicitly opts in.

Shipping it meant landing changes across three production repositories as reviewed
pull requests: the lead API and structured lead storage in the NestJS/MongoDB backend,
the calculator flow in the Next.js landing site, and the captured leads surfaced in the
React admin CMS.

*Stack: Next.js · TypeScript · Drizzle ORM · Gemini · NestJS · MongoDB · React · Vercel*
